From the Desk

The Trade in Words

Notes on financial translation that expose how big-agency “experts” often get market metaphors and terminology wrong. A boutique perspective on the small choices that actually move meaning.

The mistake looks small: one word, “put.” The damage is larger. A market metaphor was translated as a product label, and the sentence lost its trade. Once that point is missed, the translation can look technically tidy and still misprice the meaning.

Wrong track

「格林斯潘看跌期权」

Literal product label. Bearish verb tilts the wrong way.

Preferred

托市 / 包底托市

Market support. Agent and floor stay clear.
