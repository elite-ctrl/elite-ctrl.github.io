Questions about this cache

What is Bilingual filings?

Bilingual filings is a Beta desk tool on LedgerLex Language Studio. Search a Hong Kong issuer, filter HKEXnews headline types, and open the English and Traditional Chinese announcement PDFs together in one tab when both exist. Beta snapshot: 10 November 2026 (Asia/Hong_Kong). The published cache covers Main Board and GEM equity and ETF issuers from 10 November 2023 through 10 November 2026.

Does the page query HKEXnews while I search?

No. Search reads the published issuer index (stocks.json) beside this page. Choosing an issuer loads that issuer’s paired filings. It does not call HKEXnews on every click. Confirm a filing on HKEXnews before you rely on it.

Which filings are left out of the cache?

Monthly Returns (the whole Tier 1, including 月報表 and code 51500) are removed before publish. Next Day Disclosure Returns for share buybacks are removed before publish. Other Next Day Disclosure Returns stay. AGM circulars and announcements about a share repurchase stay. Debt and Structured Products (債券及結構性產品, including cancelled or reissued prefixes, and the related documents on display for debt programmes, debt securities, and structured products) are removed before publish. English stubs that only say the filing was published in Chinese are dropped, and a bilingual open needs a real PDF on both sides.

How does Bilingual filings sit with the other Tools?

Call the Ledger checks figures once the PDFs are on the desk. The Printer index is a working list of Hong Kong financial printers. The Board meeting heat map shows which days look busy. Language Watchdog is the journal of language-risk notes. Each one is optional.
