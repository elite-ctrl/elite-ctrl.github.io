Call the Gaps Beta

Call text gaps on a bilingual pack before it leaves the desk. Drop the precedent and the newer one as PDF or Word (.docx); inserts, deletes, and moves are typed across the whole document in this browser — not by page number. Unequal page counts, or PDF pages beside Word paragraphs, are expected and fine. All data stays on your machine — nothing is uploaded for the check.

Disclaimer This is a beta desk aid. Extracted text can miss scan-only PDF content, embedded images, and text boxes the reader skips. Complex layouts and other edge cases apply on both formats. Check both files yourself. Call the Gaps is not a substitute for professional review.

Your pack files stay in this browser. File contents are not uploaded to LedgerLex servers for this check. Google Analytics may still record a page view, as it does on the rest of the site. See the Privacy Policy.

Upload precedent and newer-one files (PDF or Word) to compare.

Paragraph-level alignment handles bilingual packs where wording differs but structure should track. Short inserts and typos inside a matched paragraph are marked with token-level diff when the paragraph text differs. Scanned or image-only PDFs, and text trapped in Word images without extraction, are not covered. Always run your own final check on anything flagged.
