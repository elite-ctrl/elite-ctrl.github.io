Questions about this roster

What is the Printer index?

The Printer index is a working list of Hong Kong financial printers, with website and telephone for each. Keep it to hand when you need a printer’s contact details quickly. It is a Beta tool in the same family as Call the Ledger, Call the Gaps, and the Board meeting heat map. Beta snapshot: 9 November 2026 (Asia/Hong_Kong). The list is in no particular order. It is not an exhaustive list of Hong Kong financial printers.

Is the Printer index a ranking or an endorsement?

No. The roster is for reference only. It is not a ranking, an endorsement, or an introduction service. Order on the page does not imply a preference.

How should I confirm a printer’s contact details?

Contact details change. Confirm the number and the website with the printer before you rely on them.

How does the Printer index relate to Call the Ledger, Language Watchdog, and the Board meeting heat map?

The Board meeting heat map shows which days look busy for printer operations. This roster names printers you may need to reach. Call the Ledger checks figures on a bilingual pack already on the desk. Language Watchdog is the journal of language-risk notes on filings and circulars. Each one is optional.
