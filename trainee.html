The desk

About the program

LedgerLex Language Studio is a Hong Kong language services boutique for fund houses, listed companies, and investor relations teams. Work covers fund materials, listed company reports, and corporate communications in English, Traditional Chinese, and Simplified Chinese.

The Trainee program gives careful, curious people practical experience in financial language work. Trainees support translation and proofreading under supervision, with attention to terminology, figures, formatting, and client style.

This program focuses on financial language work. The Studio’s wider desk goes beyond translation alone.

Learn the desk standard. Language that holds under pressure.

Questions

FAQ

How is the work arranged?

Part time and as needed. Some weeks are busier than others. The desk will tell you when practice is available.

Is the program remote?

Yes. Trainee practice is part time and remote, supporting a Hong Kong financial language desk.

Can career path change seekers and undergraduates apply?

Yes. Career path change seekers and undergraduates are welcome if they pass our tests. You also need the right to undertake this work in Hong Kong.

Is there a written exercise?

Shortlisted candidates may get a short written exercise. The text is provided. You do not need to bring client material.

Which languages matter most?

Strong written English and Chinese. Careful Traditional Chinese is important. Simplified Chinese is an advantage. Work spans fund materials, listed reporting, and corporate communications.

How is confidentiality handled?

Discretion with confidential material is required. Do not send confidential or client owned samples with your application. Desk materials stay inside the supervised workflow.
