# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Buyers at Hong Kong listed companies, funds, and investor-relations teams, plus other translation suppliers. The public site is how those buyers meet the desk. The Language Watchdog column speaks to peer suppliers and is also there to attract new clients.

## Product Purpose

LedgerLex Language Studio is a Hong Kong financial translation desk for funds, IPO, and IR / listed reporting. This repository is the public marketing site at https://ledgerlexlanguagestudio.net. Success on a marketing page is a buyer who understands the offer and makes contact. Success on a journal page is a reader who finishes the piece.

## Positioning

A specialist Hong Kong desk for financial language: funds, IPO, and IR / listed reporting. The public journal is Language Watchdog, an ongoing multi-entry column.

## Operating Context

The site is static HTML, English only. Marketing pages ask a visitor to engage the desk. Language Watchdog and Trade in Words are journal pages for reading.

Ready, send, price, and client mail stay with the principal alone.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- English only for now. No Traditional Chinese or Simplified Chinese mirrors, and no hreflang.
- The trainee program exists. It welcomes career-path changers and undergraduates who pass the tests. Do not publish dates or rates on the Trainee page.
- Required home document title: “Hong Kong Financial Translation — Funds, IPO, IR | LedgerLex”. The live `index.html` title already matches.
- Required Trainee document title: “Financial Language Trainee | Hong Kong Desk — LedgerLex”. The live `trainee.html` title now matches the lock.
- Public journal name: Language Watchdog.
- This pass does not redesign pages, rewrite copy, or change the Carbon Desk Chrome extension.

## Brand Commitments

Brand locks override Impeccable skill defaults.

The public site is neo-brutalist HTML: sharp type and sharp shapes. The homepage direction is centre-aligned. Visual tokens, including what the live CSS actually uses, are in `DESIGN.md`.

The homepage hero must not lead with an HKEX, listings, or filings regulatory frame. It is offer-first and creative.

Never put MotionSites ads, links, or branding into client deliverable files.

## Evidence on Hand

- Live pages in this repo: `index.html`, `financial-translation.html`, `trainee.html`, `language-watchdog.html`, `language-watchdog-cpic-2026-q3.html`, `trade-in-words.html`, `privacy.html`, `terms.html`, `404.html`.
- Every page is `lang="en"`. No hreflang links are present.
- Marks and icons live under `brand/`.
- Published page copy is already on those HTML files. Do not add testimonials, client names, rates, dates, or other claims that Louis has not locked.
- Homepage hero states each desk claim once, in the proof strip: “10 years on the desk”, “Priced before you commit”, “Short pilot, no fee”.

## Product Principles

1. The principal alone marks work ready, sends it, prices it, and writes client mail.
2. Client deliverable files stay free of MotionSites.
3. Brand locks override Impeccable skill defaults.
4. The public site stays English-only until Louis adds language mirrors.
5. The homepage hero leads with the offer. It does not open on a regulatory frame.
