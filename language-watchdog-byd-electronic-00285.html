BYD Electronic (00285): six translation pitfalls in the 2024 interim results

Language Watchdog — C2E. Traditional Chinese is the source, English is the target.

Framing

How much can a translation change without changing the message? BYD Electronic’s 2024 interim results announcement provides six examples, ranging from awkward phrasing to differences in attribution and scope. The Chinese is treated as the source for this comparison.

Direction is C2E: Traditional Chinese is the source, English is the target. The announcement was released on 28 August 2024.

Public filing — BYD Electronic (00285): EN · TC.

1. An Awkward Collocation: First-Mover Advantage

Source (TC)

本集團已佈局智能座艙系統、智能駕駛系統、智能懸架系統、熱管理、控制器和傳感器等領域，具備明顯的先發技術優勢，多個產品已實現量產交付，並持續提升出貨量。

Filing translation (EN)

The Group has deployed intelligent cockpit systems, intelligent driving systems, intelligent suspension systems, thermal management, controllers, sensors and other areas, and has obvious advantages in first mover technology. A number of products have been delivered in mass production, and the shipment volume has been continuously increased.

Better rendition

The Group has established a presence in intelligent cockpit systems, intelligent driving systems, intelligent suspension systems, thermal management, controllers, sensors and other areas, and has a clear first-mover technological advantage. Several products have entered mass production and are being delivered, with shipment volumes continuing to increase.

Analysis: “Advantages in first mover technology” is awkward: it can suggest an advantage in a category of technology called “first mover technology”. “First-mover technological advantage” more clearly connects the advantage with the Group’s early position.

2. “Leading” and “Largest”

Source (TC)

集團全面佈局了低算力、中算力、高算力等智駕平台，智能駕駛產品在國內市場的出貨量持續領先。

Filing translation (EN)

The Group has fully deployed intelligent driving platforms (including low, medium and high computing power platforms) to facilitate intelligent driving products in recording the continuous largest shipments in the domestic market.

Better rendition

The Group has established a comprehensive presence in intelligent driving platforms with low, medium and high computing power, and its intelligent driving products have consistently maintained a leading position in the domestic market in terms of shipment volume.

Analysis: “The continuous largest shipments” is awkward, and “largest” makes an explicit ranking claim. 「持續領先」 can indicate market leadership, but does not by itself establish whether the company means an unqualified number-one position. The English also presents the deployment as a means to an end (“to facilitate”), a relationship not explicitly stated in the Chinese.

3. Whose Forecast?

Source (TC)

IDC的報告指出，隨着生成式人工智能（Gen AI）日趨盛行，為沉寂已久的智能手機市場注入新動能，看好AI手機市況將大爆發，估計二零二四年全年AI手機出貨量按年增長364%至2.34億部，到二零二八年其全球出貨量將達9.12億部，二零二三至二零二八年的年均複合增長率為78.4%。

Filing translation (EN)

IDC’s report points out that with the increasing prevalence of generative AI (Gen AI), the stagnant smartphone market will be injected with new vitality, we foresee a significant surge in the AI smartphone market. AI smartphone shipments are expected to grow by 364% year-on-year to 234 million units in 2024, reaching 912 million units globally by 2028, with a compound annual growth rate of 78.4% from 2023 to 2028.

Better rendition

IDC’s report noted that the growing prevalence of generative AI (Gen AI) was bringing fresh momentum to the long-stagnant smartphone market and that the AI smartphone market was expected to grow rapidly. It estimated that AI smartphone shipments would increase by 364% year-on-year to 234 million units in 2024 and reach 912 million units globally by 2028, representing a compound annual growth rate of 78.4% from 2023 to 2028.

Analysis: The Chinese presents the outlook within IDC’s report. “We foresee” introduces a first-person voice that could be read as the Group’s own forecast, making the attribution unclear. The comma before it also creates a run-on sentence.

4. An Added Qualifier: “High Growth Potential”

Source (TC)

未來，依託世界一流的研發實力、全球化佈局和垂直整合優勢，集團亦將持續開拓具備增長潛力的新品類和新市場，帶動業務長期可持續發展。

Filing translation (EN)

In the future, leveraging its world-class R&D capabilities, global presence and advantages of vertical integration, the Group will also remain committed to developing new categories and markets with high growth potential, thereby introducing impetus to the sustainable development of its business.

Better rendition

Looking ahead, the Group will also continue to leverage its world-class R&D capabilities, global presence and vertical integration advantages to develop new categories and markets with growth potential, supporting the long-term sustainable development of its business.

Analysis: The Chinese describes new categories and markets with growth potential. Adding “high” gives that potential a degree not stated in the source.

5. The Missing Segment Qualifier

Source (TC)

二零二四年下半年，隨著進入消費電子的年度需求旺季，集團的消費電子的業務表現將更為亮眼。

Filing translation (EN)

In the second half of 2024, as demand for consumer electronics enters the peak season during the year, the electronics business of the Group will achieve more outstanding performance.

Better rendition

In the second half of 2024, as consumer electronics enters its annual peak demand season, the Group’s consumer electronics business will deliver stronger performance.

Analysis: The English mentions consumer electronics earlier in the sentence, so the intended segment may be clear from context. Repeating “consumer” before “electronics business”, however, retains the Chinese qualifier and makes the scope explicit.

6. What Has Market Potential?

Source (TC)

本集團前瞻佈局的AIDC、機器人、智能家居、遊戲硬件、無人機等領域的市場潛力巨大。

Filing translation (EN)

The Group’s forward-looking investments in AIDC, robots, smart home, game hardware, and unmanned aerial vehicles reveal substantial market potential.

Better rendition

The areas in which the Group has positioned itself in anticipation of future opportunities, including AIDC, robotics, smart home, game hardware and unmanned aerial vehicles, have substantial market potential.

Analysis: The Chinese describes the market potential of the areas in which the Group has positioned itself. The English instead says that its investments “reveal” that potential. Restating the subject avoids introducing that additional relationship.

Core Takeaways for Financial Translators

  • Preserve attribution and scope: Keep forecasts with their source and retain business-segment qualifiers.
  • Check the subject and its relationship to the claim: Make sure the English says the same thing about the same entity.
  • Respect established collocations: Anchor terminology to recognised business and market idioms (first-mover advantage, economies of scale).
  • Adjust grammatical form: Nominalisation and shifts in parts of speech can bridge differences between Chinese and English expression, as in rendering 「持續提升出貨量」 as “with shipment volumes continuing to increase”. Use them where they produce more natural English while preserving the source meaning.
  • Check the strength of each claim: Do not add qualifiers or an explicit ranking that the source does not establish. Where the Chinese is ambiguous, preserve the ambiguity or seek clarification.
